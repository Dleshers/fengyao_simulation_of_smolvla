# Pixel review

- Four PNGs are 1920×1080 RGB lossless renders.
- All four frames contain non-uniform simulator pixels; none is blank or a placeholder.
- A single camera pose, target, crop, resolution, and lighting configuration is used for all states.
- The scripted waypoint deltas are recorded in `contact_recovery_sequence/metadata.json`; the four states differ by robot/held-peg placement.
- No labels, captions, arrows, grids, or manual pixel edits are present in the raw four PNGs.
- The sequence is explicitly conceptual and is not a replay of a Formal64 trajectory.
