# Isaac Sim conceptual contact-recovery illustrations

These four lossless PNGs are rendered from the Isaac Lab Factory peg-in-hole scene. They are a scripted conceptual sequence showing: lateral offset, fixture contact with misalignment, lateral recovery, and aligned insertion. The robot, grasped peg, hole fixture, table, lighting, and camera are simulator assets; no AI-generated pixels or manual overlays are used.
