# Near-rim contact-misalignment pair

This pair replaces the exaggerated 99 mm illustration. It is designed to show
the required contact-stage condition: the 8 mm peg remains at the hole mouth,
the lateral offset is only a few millimetres, and the peg has not been moved
far away from the fixture.

- `02_contact_misalignment.png`: Panda joint 1 offset by `+0.015 rad`; the
  measured fingertip lateral displacement is approximately `8.3 mm`, matching
  the scale at which an 8 mm peg contacts the hole rim.
- `03_lateral_recovery.png`: recorded joint state restored; measured lateral
  displacement returns to zero.

Both images use the same close camera and lighting and retain the wrist,
end-effector, peg, fixture, and hole. They are scripted conceptual
illustrations, not Formal64 trajectories, policy outputs, or quantitative
results. Place them side by side to show the contact-misalignment to recovery
transition.
