# Contact-stage rim-interference pair

This pair is a close simulator-rendered illustration of the required
contact-stage condition. The 8 mm peg centre is 6 mm from the 8 mm hole
centre in `02_contact_misalignment.png`; this gives an estimated 2 mm radial
rim interference while the peg remains at the hole mouth. In
`03_lateral_recovery.png` the peg is recentered to the hole axis from the
same camera and scene state.

The pair is intended to make “接触到但没有进入孔中” visible. It is a
scripted geometric still, not a Formal64 policy trajectory, not a measured
contact-force trace, and not quantitative evidence for tactile superiority.
The contact proxy and exact offsets are recorded in `metadata.json`.
